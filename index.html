01 — About

Applied AI, shipped to production.

I'm a Full-Stack Developer specialising in applied AI. At Jicama Tech I'm the engineer behind multiple production platforms — owning the backend, frontend, and the AI layer. I love turning fuzzy business workflows into natural-language experiences: agentic assistants that actually do things through tool-calling, vision models that read receipts and documents, and retrieval systems that make data answerable.

Beyond the model layer, I ship the whole thing — typed NestJS APIs, React front-ends, multi-tenant architecture, payments, and the DevOps to keep it live (Nginx, PM2, systemd on Linux VPS). I care about clean architecture, cost-aware model routing, and systems that don't fall over.
