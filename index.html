01 — About

Applied AI, shipped to production.

I'm a Full-Stack Developer specialising in applied AI. At Jicama Tech I'm the sole engineer behind five live platforms — KiosCart (commerce & POS), EventSH (event management), SingAdvisor (training & events), PillGit (clinic & patient care) and the jicama.tech site — owning the backend, the frontend, the mobile builds and the AI layer.

My AI work is practical: router-and-specialist assistants that actually do things through function calling, vision models that turn a floor-plan photo into an editable venue layout, JSON-mode extractors that import an event from a URL, and cost-aware provider fallback so it all stays up. Around the models I ship typed NestJS APIs, React front-ends, Razorpay & PayNow payments, WhatsApp automation on Baileys, and the DevOps — Nginx, PM2, Docker, GitHub-Actions auto-deploy — that keeps everything live.
